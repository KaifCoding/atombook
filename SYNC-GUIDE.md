# Use AtomBook on your phone and computer

AtomBook can keep your invoices, customers and items the same on every device you use. You
sign in with Google once, and AtomBook saves a copy of your data in a **hidden folder in your own
Google Drive**. Nobody at Atomprod can see it, and AtomBook cannot open any of your other Drive files.

It still works with no internet. When you are back online it syncs by itself.

## Turn on sync (2 minutes)

1. Open AtomBook and go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Google Drive sync** and tap **Connect Google Drive**.
3. Choose your Google account and tap **Allow**. Google will say AtomBook wants to
   "see, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive". That is the hidden
   folder. It is the only permission AtomBook asks for.
4. You will see **Connected** with your email and "Last synced: just now".

Do the same on your other phone or computer, using the **same Google account**.

> On Android and Windows, Google sign-in opens in your normal browser and then brings you back
> to AtomBook. That is normal. Google does not allow sign-in inside other apps.

## Your second device

- **A new device with no data:** after you connect, your data appears automatically.
- **A device that already has its own invoices:** AtomBook asks you to choose, because two
  separate sets of invoices cannot be mixed safely (invoice numbers would clash).
  - *Use the Google Drive data on this device*: this device takes the data from Drive.
  - *Keep this device's data and replace Google Drive*: this device's data becomes the main copy.
  - Tap **Download a backup of this device first** if you are unsure. It saves a file you can restore later.

## Everyday use

- Just work as usual. Changes are sent a few seconds after you save.
- The small chip at the bottom of the menu (or top of the screen on a phone) shows the status:
  *Synced just now*, *Syncing…*, *Offline: will sync later*, or *Reconnect Google*.
- **Reconnect Google** appears when Google signs you out (the web version does this about once an
  hour). Tap it once and carry on. Nothing is lost.
- If you edit the same invoice or customer on two devices, the **most recent edit** is kept.
- If two devices were offline and both issued the same invoice number, the later one gets the next
  free number and AtomBook tells you. Invoice numbers are never duplicated.

## Stop syncing

- **Disconnect** signs this device out of Google. Your data stays on the device.
- **Delete Drive copy** removes AtomBook's files from your Google Drive and turns sync off
  everywhere. The data already on your devices is kept.
- You can also remove access any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Good to know

- Sync copies changes, including mistakes. If you delete a customer by accident on one device, it
  is deleted on the others too. Keep making a **backup** now and then (Settings → Backup).
- Keep your devices' date and time on **automatic**. Sync uses the time of each edit.
- If something looks wrong, tap **Sync now** in Settings. Still stuck? Write to
  [kaif@atomprod.in](mailto:kaif@atomprod.in) with the message you see.

## Questions

**Is my data safe?** It stays on your devices and in your own Google Drive. It does not go through
any server of ours. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://atombook.atomprod.in/privacy/).

**Do I need to pay or make an account?** No. You only need a Google account if you want sync.

**Can I use it without sync?** Yes. Sync is optional. Without it, your data stays on the device
and you can move it with a backup file.

**Why does Google say "unverified app"?** Only if the app has not finished Google's checks yet.
If you trust the app, choose *Advanced* and continue. AtomBook only asks for its own hidden folder.
